HOW TO PLAY

THE TURN

  1. A shell is rolled for you. Its weight changes everything.
  2. Read the wind at the top of the screen.
  3. Set elevation with W / S and charge with A / D.
  4. Press SPACE to start loading, then work the three stages.
  5. The round goes wherever your hands sent it.

THE THREE STAGES

RAM THE CHARGE
Hammer SPACE. More strokes means more propellant, which means more range. A fumbled ram costs about a fifth of your charge.
LAY THE TUBE
Stop the marker on the centre mark. Off-centre throws your elevation by up to eight degrees.
SET THE FUSE
Freeze the pulsing ring on the sight ring. A tight fuse bursts the shell as it passes near the enemy instead of sailing on.

WEIGHT IS THE WHOLE GAME

Light shells fly furthest and hit hardest at the exact centre of the blast, but their lethal radius is tiny and the wind throws them around.

Heavy shells barely cross a wide field and cap out lower, but their blast forgives a sloppy round and the wind hardly touches them.

You do not choose which one you get. You choose what to do with it.

ANGLE IS A TRADE

Forty-five degrees squeezes the most range out of a charge and is the angle where a shaky tube costs you least. It is also the slowest shot on the board, so it hands the wind the most time to work.

A flat, fast round beats the wind and punishes a bad elevation lock. Pick your poison.

THE GROUND

Every round takes a bite out of the map. Undercut a shelf and the whole overhang comes down. If the ground under a gun goes, so does the gun, and the fall hurts.

PRACTISING ALONE

PRACTICE VS AI gives you a computer gunner at four levels. It does not cheat: it solves the same shot you do and works the same three stages. What its level mostly changes is how badly it misreads the wind, which is the same mistake you are learning not to make.

MUSTERING

The game could not start.

The most likely cause is a browser extension. Ad blockers and privacy extensions block scripts by filename, and one blocked file stops the whole game loading. Try pausing the extension for this page and reloading.

Failing that: check you are online, and that a corporate network or VPN is not filtering the connection.
